# LunaCore Branding Policy 1.0

The names **LunaCore**, **LunaNPC** and every other LunaCore product name, their logos, and the
domains lunacore.dev and lunanpc.dev (the "Marks") belong to Danijel Susak, operating under the
name LunaCore. The LunaCore Product License does not license them. This policy says how you may
use them; it does not limit what the law allows anyway, such as naming a product to describe
what your own work is compatible with. Capitalised terms (Official Release, Origin Notices, Private Use)
mean what the LunaCore Product License defines.

## Allowed without asking

- **Stating truthfully what you use or build on**, in text: "runs on LunaNPC", "compatible with
  LunaNPC", "an add-on for LunaNPC", "uses the LunaNPC API", "based on LunaNPC by LunaCore". This
  includes the description of a paid add-on or service, as long as the Mark is not part of its
  name or logo.
- **Labelling shared Official Releases** with the product's name, version, "by LunaCore" and a
  link, as the product license requires (modpacks, server packs, install lists).
- Showing an **unmodified official logo** next to such a label or link, or in a video, article,
  review, tutorial or server list about the product, including paid content.
- Keeping the Marks in Origin Notices, as the product license requires.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using a Mark, or anything confusingly similar ("LunaNPC Pro", "LunaNPC 2", "Luna-NPC",
  "NanoNPC" with a look-alike logo), in the **name, logo or domain** of your own product,
  project, mod, server or service.
- Suggesting that LunaCore made, endorses, sponsors, certifies or supports you or your work.
- Changing, animating, recolouring or cropping the logos, or combining them with other marks.
- Putting the Marks on merchandise or on anything sold, other than the truthful text statements
  above.
- Registering a Mark or anything confusingly similar as a trademark, company name, domain,
  social media handle or app name.

## Forks

A fork kept for Private Use or for contributions keeps the original name and Origin Notices. A
fork may not be published under the original name or under a new one; publishing a changed
version needs a license under the LunaCore Product License.

## Requests and reports

Ask for permission or report misuse at https://lunacore.dev/support. LunaCore may change this
policy at any time; the current version is in each product's repository.
